# Roadmap

Alquerque is a 5×5 slide game in the browser. Two players start with twelve pieces each, the centre empty, Dark slides first to an empty neighbour, a jump over an opponent takes that piece, a landing that can take again keeps the turn, a jump that is open must be taken, a colour that cannot keep playing loses, legal points are marked, and a ply can be undone. About 35 hours remain.

## Later work

These stay as later work. They are about 35 hours.

### Opening book (4 hours)

Suggest a first-slide reply from a small set of known openings.

### Difficulty (3 hours)

Offer an easy or a medium opponent that looks one jump ahead.

### Clocks (3 hours)

An optional timer on each colour's turn.

### Capture animation (3 hours)

Lift a jumped piece off the board instead of clearing it at once.

### Save and load (3 hours)

Write a position to a local file and restore it.

### Match of three (3 hours)

Play until one colour wins two games, and keep that tally.

### Print diagram (3 hours)

Lay the board and the move list out for one printed page.

### Seven-by-seven board (4 hours)

Widen the array to seven files and seven ranks.

### Spanish kings (3 hours)

Let a piece that reaches the far rank step backward as a king.

### Keyboard (3 hours)

Move a cursor between points with the arrows and slide with Enter.

### Drag pieces (3 hours)

Drag a piece to an empty neighbour.

An opening book is the next piece. The game can slide, take by a jump, chain takes, force a take, win, hint, and undo, and it does not yet suggest an opening reply.
